## Honest read

Most ranked pairs sit on n = 5–9 games, where a single win or loss swings synergy by ~0.05–0.09 — suggestive only. Restricting to n >= 10 thins the field hard on a 539-game patch: few pairs clear that bar, and the ones that do are mostly high-pick-rate meta champions whose 'synergy' partly reflects being strong individually that patch. The 16.17 overlap check shows pair effects do not persist patch to patch. Antagonistic pairs cluster in two recognizable types: (1) double-carry bot lanes (two ADCs / two mages) that starve each other of farm and lack engage, and (2) conflicting-engage comps (e.g. poke + hard-engage support) where the pair's game plans fight each other. Both are more draft-coherence stories than true anti-synergy, and both are small-sample. Bottom line: pair terms are descriptive color, not model features — consistent with the dropped d_synergy experiment. (Note: the experiment's "Xayah+Rakan <5 games on 16.18" referred to the walk-forward prior available at each prediction time; the full-patch descriptive count here is 44.)
