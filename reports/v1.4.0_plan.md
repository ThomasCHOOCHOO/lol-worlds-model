## What the evidence says, in one paragraph each

**Counterpicks (1) and pooled matchup edges (2) fail for the same reason:**
the signal is real but small, and matchup history goes stale faster than it
accumulates. Patches rework the game; a counter that was +0.10 last split is a
coin flip this split. The lane-by-lane follow-up (2b) found the exception:
top-lane edges predict top gold well (R² 0.113), but top gold is the *least*
valuable gold — bot gold matters most and is least predictable from draft.
That predictability-vs-value asymmetry is the structural reason draft-matchup
features keep failing the gate.

**Momentum (3):** the only real signal is the previous map's winner, and even
that is confounded — it could be psychology, latent form, or draft adaptation,
and the data cannot tell them apart. Score state and tournament streaks are
noise. Ship prev-win at half strength (+0.14) inside the series-state model,
games 2+ only, never alongside score dummies, and kill it if the
pre-registered Worlds validation fails.

**Player Elo (4):** the most instructive failure. It wins on the pooled tail
because minor leagues dominate the game count — but in tier-1 +
internationals, where the bets are, it is *significantly worse*. Team Elo
captures a real org component (coaching, infrastructure, cohesion) that
player Elo discards, and player Elo leaks former-team strength into new
teams. The KeSPA Cup case (academy squads under main-org names, 40% of the
pooled gain from 1.8% of games) proves the failure mode is real — just not
the regime that matters. Path forward is a tier-conditional blend, not a
global one: M2 proved a single linear blend cannot learn "player Elo in
unstable contexts, team Elo in stable ones." Cheapest win: a diagnostic
flag when |player − team| gap > 100 — zero model risk, catches the blowups.

**Region offsets (5):** the clearest win of the batch. Elo overrates weak
regions and underrates LCK by ~178 Elo points. Vietnam is biased, not a wild
card. Offsets are constant (decay tested and rejected) and must be refit on
every refresh (LPL's edge shrank ~40% from 2024 to 2025–26).

**Series with state (6):** the machinery is designed and prototyped. It
replaces the binomial independence assumption with a Bayesian update between
maps (live closing estimate enters as a weight, not a second observation;
draft enters separately). Needs: prev-win parameter from (3b), and app
wiring — series-score tracking, closing-estimate capture, champions-used
per team (Fearless).

**Honest calibration:** the shipped 0.000 calibration error is an artifact of
grading the map on the games that built it. The honest test showed a stale
map can hurt (0.682 vs 0.649 raw). v1.4.0 must evaluate calibration on
untouched games (nested splits), clip predictions away from 0/100%, and use
fewer, wider calibration steps.

**Role-weighted live gold:** ADC gold dominates, support gold is ~worthless
— exactly as the lane analysis predicted. Lift is small and mid-game
concentrated; only t=20 clears the gate on the honest split. Ship variant A
(roles replace total; variant B is a reparameterization, do not ship) after
a shadow test on live Worlds Swiss games.

**Objective timers:** the Leaguepedia pipeline works (1,299-game pilot,
30,439 events). The first-objective → gold → win analysis is the remaining
step for this data, plus the 11–12h backfill and the LPL unblock.

## Phase 1 — implement (needs go-ahead)

1. **Region offsets** (`src/region_offsets.py`, `elo_diff_adj` in
   `build_features.py`, refit in `refresh.py`). Gate: production-config
   backtest (offsets folded into `elo_diff` + model refit) must confirm
   the standalone ΔLL ≈ −0.05.
2. **Honest calibration**: nested-split evaluation in `train.py`, prediction
   clipping, fewer calibration steps. Gate: honest ECE/log-loss reported
   on untouched games for every retrain.
3. **Series-state model** (`src/model/seriesState.ts`): implement the design
   from `reports/series_state_design.md` with prev-win +0.14, games 2+ only.
   Gates: full-model re-test of prev-win (draft features may absorb it);
   pre-registered validation on Worlds 2026 Play-In + Swiss games 2+.
4. **Role-weighted live model, variant A**: re-run the export step that
   serializes fitted isotonic wrappers (the JSON written is
   metrics/coefficients only, not deployable as-is); `live.ts` reads five
   role-gold fields; keep baseline for OCR games. Gate: shadow-test on
   live Worlds Swiss games — revert if realized log-loss ≤ baseline.
5. **Player–team diagnostic flag** (cheap, zero model risk): compute
   |player − team| gap at prediction time; warn when > 100. No model
   change; app display only.

## Phase 2 — leads to re-test (not in v1.4.0)

- Top-only matchup feature on a fresh post-Worlds holdout, pre-registered.
- Tier-conditional player blend: needs a roster-instability context variable
  (lineup vs trailing-30-day modal lineup) + fit blend weight + tier-1
  non-inferiority bar. Do not attempt as a global blend — M2 already
  failed that.
- Glicko-style RD: attach uncertainty to ratings (new teams, weak-region
  teams, roster changes); use it to temper predictions, not as a feature
  until tested.
- Re-validate `roster_strength.py` on tier-1+intl (same tier dependence
  as player Elo likely applies).

## What v1.4.0 is NOT

Counterpick features, pooled matchup edges, series-score dummies,
tournament streak features, player Elo as a feature, live-model variant B,
slope shrinkage on Elo, decayed region offsets, 2v2 bot-pair features.
