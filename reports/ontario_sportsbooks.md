## List 1 — Top 5 Ontario-licensed books for LoL

### 1. Pinnacle (pinnacle.ca) — the sharp book
- **License:** AGCO / iGaming Ontario since Oct 2022. Also licensed in Malta, Curaçao.
- **LoL coverage:** Esports pioneer (offered since 2010); full Worlds coverage — match winner, map handicaps, totals, live in-play.
- **Margins:** ~2% vs industry 5–7% — consistently the best price in Canada.
- **Limits:** High limits; **"Winners Welcome" — famously does NOT limit or ban winning bettors.** The only book where a profitable model bettor is safe long-term.
- **Rewards:** None — no welcome bonus, ever. The low margin IS the reward.
- **Drawbacks:** No mobile app (mobile site only); no Interac; only one free withdrawal per month; no live streaming.

### 2. bet365 — deepest LoL markets
- **License:** AGCO / iGaming Ontario; largest customer base in the province.
- **LoL coverage:** Best market depth of any licensed book — match winner, map handicaps, total maps, first blood, first dragon, total barons/towers, team-first props; live in-play with real-time stats and live streaming; full/partial cash-out.
- **Margins:** ~5–7% typical.
- **Limits:** **Known to aggressively limit winning accounts** — consistently beating the closing line or steady profits will get stakes cut (sometimes to single dollars). Use for market depth and live betting, expect a short shelf life as a winner.
- **Rewards:** Recurring promos; no headline welcome bonus in Ontario (bonus structure varies — check current offer).
- **Drawbacks:** The account-limiting risk above; withdrawals 12h–5 business days depending on method.

### 3. Rivalry — esports-first, Ontario-only
- **License:** AGCO / iGaming Ontario; Canadian-owned, HQ in Toronto.
- **LoL coverage:** Esports is the core product — 20+ esports titles, 50+ markets on big LoL matches, live betting, live streaming, early cash-out, "Quick Bet".
- **Margins:** Recreational pricing, softer than Pinnacle.
- **Limits:** No widespread reputation for limiting winners; smaller book, lower default limits than Pinnacle/bet365.
- **Rewards:** Boosted odds on select games; esports-focused promos.
- **Drawbacks:** Odds quality trails the sharp books; traditional sports coverage is thin (irrelevant here). iOS + Android apps available.

### 4. Betway — strong esports hub
- **License:** AGCO / iGaming Ontario (Super Group).
- **LoL coverage:** Dedicated esports hub with stats and match info; solid LoL pre-match and live coverage.
- **Margins:** Standard recreational (~5–7%).
- **Limits:** Recreational book — sharp winners can expect eventual limits, though less documented than bet365.
- **Rewards:** Welcome offer exists but carries **10x wagering on bonus at odds ≥1.75** (steep); regular boosts for existing users.
- **Drawbacks:** Bonus terms are poor value for a model bettor; 25-sport menu is lighter than bet365.

### 5. BetRivers — best loyalty program
- **License:** AGCO / iGaming Ontario (Rush Street Interactive).
- **LoL coverage:** Carries LoL, CS2, Valorant, Dota 2; moneyline/totals plus live betting and streaming; shallower prop menu than bet365.
- **Margins:** Fair/industry-standard.
- **Limits:** Standard recreational policy.
- **Rewards:** **iRush Rewards** — points on virtually every wager, redeemable for bonus bets and odds boosts; one of the better loyalty programs in North American betting.
- **Drawbacks:** Esports is a side menu, not a specialty; app highly rated (4.9 App Store) but esports depth is the weakest of this five.

**Honourable mention:** DraftKings carries LoL in Ontario with decent market depth and strong promos, but pricing is rarely the best — fine as a 6th account for line shopping.
